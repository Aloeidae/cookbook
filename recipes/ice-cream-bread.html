Melted ice cream plus self-rising flour equals, somehow, bread. It shouldn't work, it does work, and the ice cream you pick is the entire recipe — so pick the full-fat one with things in it.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease a loaf pan (8×4 inch) and line with parchment. Let the ice cream melt to a pourable, milkshake-adjacent state — on the counter, like a person with patience, or gently in the microwave.

  2. 02

    Stir the flour into the melted ice cream just until combined. The batter will be thick and a little lumpy. Resist improving it — this recipe has two ingredients and zero need for your help.

  3. 03

    Scrape into the pan, smooth the top, and scatter on chips or sprinkles if using. Bake 40–45 minutes, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.

  4. 04

    Cool 10 minutes, then lift out and slice. It's a dense, tender quick bread that tastes like whatever the ice cream was. Serve toasted with butter — or, in a move nobody can stop you from making, with a scoop of ice cream.
