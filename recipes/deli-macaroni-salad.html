The New York deli-counter classic, from Sip and Feast. The secret isn't the mayo — it's the sweet-sour onion brine the macaroni sleeps in overnight. Mayo comes last, and only after the brine has done its work.

Ingredients

  • For the brine
  • For the salad

Method

  1. 01

    Whisk the vinegar, water, sugar, oil, grated onion, salt, and white pepper in a large bowl until the sugar dissolves. That's the brine — the whole deli flavor lives here.

  2. 02

    Boil the macaroni in salted water to al dente. Drain and cool it completely under cold running water — warm pasta would drink the brine unevenly.

  3. 03

    Re-whisk the brine, then add the macaroni, bell pepper, and celery. Toss until every elbow is coated. Cover and refrigerate overnight — this is not optional; it's the recipe.

  4. 04

    Next day, stir in the mayonnaise until creamy, adding more a spoonful at a time if it looks tight. Taste, adjust salt and pepper, and serve cold.
