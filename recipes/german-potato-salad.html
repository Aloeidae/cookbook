The warm one: no mayo, just bacon fat, cider vinegar, and mustard soaked into still-hot potatoes. Dress them warm or don't bother — cold potatoes refuse to drink.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Boil the potatoes whole in well-salted water until a knife slides in without resistance, 15–20 minutes depending on size. Drain, and as soon as you can handle them, slice into ½-inch rounds.

  2. 02

    Meanwhile, cook the bacon in a wide skillet over medium heat until crisp. Bacon out, fat stays — you want about 3 tablespoons in the pan.

  3. 03

    Soften the onion in the bacon fat, 3–4 minutes. Add the vinegar, water, sugar, and mustard and simmer a minute, scraping up the bacon fond. Season assertively.

  4. 04

    Add the warm potato slices to the pan and turn gently until coated. Off the heat, let them sit 5 minutes to drink up the dressing.

  5. 05

    Fold in the bacon and most of the herbs, taste for salt and vinegar, and scatter the rest on top. Serve warm or room temperature — never fridge-cold.
