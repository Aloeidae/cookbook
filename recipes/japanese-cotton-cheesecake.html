The jiggly Osaka-style cheesecake — barely sweet, impossibly light, wobbling like it's alive. Sized for a 6 × 3-inch round tin (scale by tin volume, and the taller the tin, the more jiggle). Adapted from Mai Cookbook, Uncle Rikuro style.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Heat the oven to 390°F (200°C). Line the bottom of a 6 × 3-inch round tin with parchment and butter the sides. Boil a kettle for the water bath. No springform — or wrap the base watertight in foil.

  2. 02

    Gently warm the butter with half the sugar, then blend in the cream cheese until completely smooth. Beat in the yolks one at a time, then the milk.

  3. 03

    Sift the flour and cornstarch over the batter and mix until silky and lump-free.

  4. 04

    Whisk the whites with the cream of tartar, adding the remaining sugar gradually, until the meringue holds stiff peaks — about 5 minutes.

  5. 05

    Fold a third of the meringue into the batter to loosen it, then fold in the rest gently — it should still feel airy when it pours. Into the tin, leaving ¾ inch of headroom; set the tin in a bath of hot water reaching halfway up its side.

  6. 06

    Bake 15 minutes at 390°F, drop to 285°F (140°C) for 30 minutes, then switch the oven off and leave the cake inside, door closed, 30 minutes more. If the top browns too fast or cracks, your oven runs hot — turn it down. Unmold, jiggle, serve warm or chilled.
