Tall, fluffy, drive-thru-style buttermilk biscuits — soft inside, golden on top, brushed with butter while hot. Cold butter and a light hand do all the work; everything else is just not overthinking it.

Ingredients

  • For the dough
  • To finish

Method

  1. 01

    Heat the oven to 450°F. Whisk the flour, baking powder, baking soda, sugar, and salt. Cut in the cold butter with your fingertips or a pastry cutter until it looks like coarse crumbs with some pea-sized flakes left.

  2. 02

    Pour in the buttermilk and stir with a fork just until a shaggy dough forms. It should look rough and slightly sticky — smooth dough means tough biscuits.

  3. 03

    Turn it onto a floured counter and pat into a rectangle about ¾ inch thick. Fold it in thirds like a letter, pat out, and repeat twice more. Those folds become the flaky layers.

  4. 04

    Pat to 1 inch thick and cut with a 2½-inch cutter — press straight down, no twisting, or the edges seal and the biscuits won't rise. Re-pat scraps once. Arrange on a baking sheet with sides just touching.

  5. 05

    Bake 12–15 minutes, until tall and deeply golden on top. Brush with melted butter the second they come out. Eat warm, split, with honey running into the cracks.
