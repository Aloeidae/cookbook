The smoky-sweet dipping sauce in the Red Robin style: mayo, BBQ sauce, and a hit of chipotle, stirred together in the time it takes fries to cool. Your BBQ sauce is the whole personality here — use one you'd eat off a spoon.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Whisk the mayo, BBQ sauce, chipotle, adobo, and garlic powder in a bowl until completely smooth and terracotta-colored. Taste — more chipotle for smoke and heat, more BBQ sauce for sweet.

  2. 02

    Rest it 30 minutes in the fridge if you can wait. The smoke settles in and the whole thing tastes like it took effort.

  3. 03

    Serve with fries, onion rings, or slathered on a burger. Keeps up to a week in a sealed jar in the fridge — it will not last that long.
