A meal-prep bowl from ice.karimcooks on Instagram: crispy roasted potatoes under taco beef made saucy with Laughing Cow wedges melted right into the pan. The caption came to us incomplete, so the amounts and toppings here are our sensible fill-in.

Method

  1. 01

    Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the diced potatoes with the oil, salt, and pepper on a sheet pan and roast 25–30 minutes, flipping once, until browned and crisp at the edges.

  2. 02

    Meanwhile, brown the beef in a large skillet over medium-high, breaking it up as it goes. Lean beef won't need draining; that's the point of the 93%.

  3. 03

    Stir in the taco seasoning, then the broth, and drop in the cheese wedges. Simmer a few minutes, stirring, until they melt into a light, creamy sauce that clings to the beef.

  4. 04

    Build the bowls: potatoes on the bottom, saucy beef over, then lettuce, tomato, cheddar, a spoon of Greek yogurt, and salsa. Meal-prepping? Box the potatoes and beef together, keep the cold toppings separate, and it reheats clean for four lunches.
