No roux, no baking, no broken sauce — a Modernist Cuisine trick by way of Sip and Feast. A tablespoon of sodium citrate lets real sharp cheddar melt like the plastic-wrapped stuff, minus the plastic.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Boil the pasta in salted water to al dente. Reserve a cup of the cooking water, then drain.

  2. 02

    In a large pot, bring the milk, water, and sodium citrate to a simmer. Add the cheddar and Pecorino a handful at a time, stirring each addition smooth before the next. An immersion blender fixes any doubts.

  3. 03

    Stir in the mustard powder; season with salt and pepper. Too thin? Simmer a few minutes until it coats a spoon.

  4. 04

    Toss the pasta with half the sauce first, then add more until it's as saucy as you like — extra sauce goes on the side, and pasta water loosens it if it tightens up. Serve immediately.
