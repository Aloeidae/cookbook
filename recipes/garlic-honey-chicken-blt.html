A BLT that got ambitious, from donaldo_cooks on Instagram: cornflake-crusted air-fryer chicken thigh, garlic honey glaze over the crunch, and chili crunch mayo on the bread. The original caption was partial, so the quantities here are our fill-in.

Ingredients

  • For the chicken
  • Garlic honey glaze
  • To build

Method

  1. 01

    Season the thighs with salt, pepper, and paprika. Dredge flour, then egg, then crushed cornflakes, pressing the flakes on hard so the crust commits.

  2. 02

    Air fry at 400°F, sprayed well on both sides, 12–14 minutes, flipping halfway, until deep golden and cooked through (165°F). Crisp the bacon while it goes.

  3. 03

    The glaze: melt the butter, bloom the garlic 30 seconds, then honey and vinegar. Simmer one minute until it ribbons. Brush it over the hot chicken — glaze on crust, not crust in glaze.

  4. 04

    Stir the chili crunch into the mayo. Build: mayo on both toasted faces, lettuce, tomato, bacon, glazed chicken, top on. Napkins are not optional.
