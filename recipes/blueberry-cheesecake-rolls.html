Soft yeasted rolls swirled with cheesecake filling and jammy blueberry sauce, from a little calm's bake-with-me. Heavy cream poured over before baking is what keeps them cinnamon-roll soft.

Ingredients

  • For the dough
  • For the blueberry sauce
  • For the cheesecake filling
  • For baking & frosting

Method

  1. 01

    Stir the yeast and its sugar into the warm water and rest 10 minutes, until bubbly. No bubbles, new yeast — don't argue with it.

  2. 02

    Mix the yeast mixture with the flour, melted butter, egg and yolk, salt, sugar, and vanilla; knead about 5 minutes. It's a sticky dough — oil your hands, ball it into a bowl, cover, and let rise 1 hour.

  3. 03

    Meanwhile, simmer the blueberries, lemon juice, sugar, cornstarch, and water over medium heat until the berries soften and the sauce just thickens. Set aside to cool. Beat the filling: cream cheese, sugar, vanilla.

  4. 04

    Roll the dough to about ¼ inch thick. Spread all the cheesecake filling, then ¼ cup plus 2 tbsp of the blueberry sauce — save the rest. Roll up and cut into 6 rolls; a floss loop cuts cleaner than a knife.

  5. 05

    Arrange in a baking dish, cover, and rest 30 minutes. Pour the heavy cream over the risen rolls, then bake at 350°F for 30–35 minutes, until golden brown.

  6. 06

    Beat the frosting smooth — cream cheese, butter, powdered sugar, vanilla, cream — and spread over the warm rolls. Spoon the leftover blueberry sauce on top.
