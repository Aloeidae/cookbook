Zach Choi's version: two colors of American cheese and a can of evaporated milk, because American cheese physically cannot break. No aged cheddar snobbery here — just the silkiest sauce you'll ever broil.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Chop or tear all the cheese into rough pieces — it melts faster and more evenly than whole slices. Boil the cavatappi to al dente and drain.

  2. 02

    Melt the butter over medium heat, then stir in the paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, and flour. Cook a minute, stirring — a seasoned roux, not raw flour.

  3. 03

    Pour in the evaporated milk, whisking, and bring to a bare simmer. Melt in half the cheese, a handful at a time, until the sauce is completely smooth.

  4. 04

    Fold the noodles through until every curl is coated. Layer into a broiler-safe pan: half the noodles, some of the remaining cheese, the rest of the noodles, the rest of the cheese on top.

  5. 05

    Broil about 10 minutes, until the top is blistered and browned in spots — but watch it, broilers have opinions. Serve immediately; this sauce is at peak silk while hot.
