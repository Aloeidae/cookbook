Classic pierogi ruskie from The Polish Chef, with the dough and filling from Susan's Cooking School. Makes about three dozen — they freeze beautifully, so shape them all.

  • For the filling
  • For the dough
  • To serve

Method

  1. 01

    Boil the potatoes whole until tender, about 30 minutes. Drain, cool, peel, and rice them into a bowl — a masher leaves lumps that tear the dough later.

  2. 02

    Sauté the onion in the butter until soft and sweet. Stir into the potatoes with the cheese, salt, and pepper. Refrigerate until firm — cold filling shapes cleanly, warm filling fights you.

  3. 03

    Whisk the flour and salt. Beat the eggs into the water, then stir the liquid in gradually. Knead on the counter just one minute, until smooth — adjust with water or flour as needed. Flour the ball, cover, rest 10–15 minutes.

  4. 04

    Roll the dough about ⅛ inch thin on a floured surface and cut 3-inch rounds. A spoonful of filling in each, fold to a half-moon, and pinch the edge sealed — no filling in the seam or they'll open in the pot. Re-roll the scraps.

  5. 05

    Boil in batches in well-salted water. When they float, give them two more minutes, then lift out with a slotted spoon.

  6. 06

    Serve as is, or pan-fry in butter until blistered gold on both sides. Caramelized onions over, sour cream alongside. Non-negotiable.
