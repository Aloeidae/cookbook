Nicole McLaughlin's Allrecipes trick for spit-style doner without the spit: spiced meat rolled paper-thin, folded up in parchment, and baked. Unravel, tear, pile onto pita.

Ingredients

  • For the meat
  • For the yogurt sauce
  • To serve

Method

  1. 01

    Oven to 400°F. Combine both meats, onion, yogurt, olive oil, and all the spices in a bowl. Mix past "combined" — knead until smooth and pasty. That texture is what slices like doner instead of crumbling like meatloaf.

  2. 02

    Divide among 4 rectangular sheets of parchment. Top each with another sheet and roll the meat out to the edges — aim for about ⅛ inch thin. Peel off the top sheet.

  3. 03

    From a short end, fold meat and parchment together in roughly 1½-inch folds, like pleating a letter. Set the folded packets on a rimmed baking sheet.

  4. 04

    Bake 30–35 minutes, draining off the fat halfway. Unravel the meat from the paper; for browned edges, give the unfolded sheets another 5–10 minutes in the oven. Sprinkle with the lemon juice.

  5. 05

    Meanwhile stir together the sauce: yogurt, lemon juice, garlic, salt. Loosen with a tablespoon or two of water if it's too thick to drizzle.

  6. 06

    Tear the meat into ragged strips. Pile onto warm pita with parsley, onion, and tomato. Yogurt sauce over everything, hot sauce if you know what's good for you.
