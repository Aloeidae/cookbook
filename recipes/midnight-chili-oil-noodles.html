Chili Oil
Noodles

Jump to recipe ↓

The noodles you make standing at the stove at midnight, half-asleep, and still get exactly right. One pot, four pantry ingredients, and the saved noodle water does all the work of a sauce.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Boil the noodles one minute short of the package time. Save a mug of the cooking water before you drain — it's the sauce's backbone.

  2. 02

    Whisk the chili crisp, vinegar, soy, and a splash of noodle water in the empty hot pot. It should look loose and glossy, not thick.

  3. 03

    Return the noodles and toss hard until every strand is lacquered, adding more noodle water a spoonful at a time if it tightens up.

  4. 04

    Into bowls. Spring onions, sesame, one more spoon of chili crisp over the top. Eat immediately, standing up if necessary.
