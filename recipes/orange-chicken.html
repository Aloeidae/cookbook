Hey It's Honeysuckle put a viral TikTok orange chicken head-to-head against Panda Express — the TikTok sauce won, and her air-fried panko chicken beat the frying too. Real orange zest and juice, no neon.

  • For the chicken
  • For the sauce

Method

  1. 01

    Toss the chicken with the egg white, soy sauce, sesame oil, salt, and pepper. The egg white is the velveting trick — it keeps the thighs silky under the crust. Let it sit while you make the sauce.

  2. 02

    In a saucepan, combine the sugar, vinegar, soy sauce, and the zest and juice of both oranges. Just before it boils, stir in the cornstarch slurry, then bring to a boil and grate in the garlic and ginger. Simmer until thick and glossy, 5–8 minutes. It should taste sharper than you want — the chicken mellows it.

  3. 03

    Dip each piece of marinated chicken in the beaten egg, then roll in panko. Space out on the air fryer rack and spray generously with oil — panko only browns where the oil touches.

  4. 04

    Air fry at 400°F for 10–12 minutes, flipping halfway, until deep golden and audibly crispy. (The TikTok original dredges in flour and deep-fries — that works too; this way you skip the oil vat.)

  5. 05

    Toss the hot chicken in the sauce until every piece glistens. Over rice, sesame seeds and green onions on top. Forget Panda.
