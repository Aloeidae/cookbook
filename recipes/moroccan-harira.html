The tomato-lentil-chickpea soup that breaks the fast across Morocco, as spotted on Jujumao's soup series — the short carries no amounts, so this is the classic, tadouira and all. The flour slurry at the end is what makes it harira — velvet, not brothy.

  • Body

Method

  1. 01

    Warm the oil in a big pot and brown the meat lightly. Add the onion, celery, and all the spices and cook 5 minutes, until fragrant and the onion has gone soft. The spices bloom in the oil now or never.

  2. 02

    Stir in the cilantro, parsley, crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, and lentils. Pour in the water, bring to a boil, then simmer partly covered about 45 minutes, until the lentils are tender and the meat gives.

  3. 03

    Chickpeas and vermicelli in. Simmer another 10 minutes. Meanwhile whisk the flour into the 1½ cups water until smooth — this is the tadouira. Let it sit while the noodles cook; some ferment it for days, you have ten minutes.

  4. 04

    Stream the tadouira into the pot, stirring constantly so it thickens without lumping. Simmer 10–15 minutes more, stirring often, until the soup turns silky and coats the spoon.

  5. 05

    Season, then ladle up with cilantro on top and lemon squeezed over. Traditionally taken with dates on the side — recommended, not required.
