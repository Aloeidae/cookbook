The pride of Pennsylvania, 1968, via B. Dylan Hollis: salty pretzel crust, sweet cream-cheese middle, ruby Jell-O top. It's not a salad. Nobody in 1968 thought it was a salad either.

Ingredients

  • For the crust
  • For the cream layer
  • For the strawberry layer

Method

  1. 01

    Oven to 350°F. Stir the pretzel crumbs with the sugar and melted butter, press into a 13×9 pan, and bake 8 minutes. Then let it cool completely — this matters later.

  2. 02

    Beat the cream cheese with the sugar and salt until smooth, then fold in the Cool Whip.

  3. 03

    Smooth the cream over the fully cooled crust, sealing it to the pan edges — that seal keeps the Jell-O from leaking under and sogging the pretzels. Put it on a warm crust and the city of Pittsburgh will throw rocks through your windows.

  4. 04

    Dissolve the Jell-O in the boiling water, then stir in the frozen strawberries. Wait until it gels but hasn't set — thick enough to mound slightly. Sometimes that's fast; sometimes it needs 10 minutes in the fridge. Don't walk away.

  5. 05

    Pour the strawberry layer gently over the cream and refrigerate overnight. Goodnight. Good morning. Slice into squares — salty, sweet, creamy, and it slices clean.
