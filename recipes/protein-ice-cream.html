Fair warning: this one requires a Ninja Creami — no machine, no dice. Five minutes of whisking today buys you a pint of vanilla–peanut-butter-cup ice cream tomorrow.

Ingredients

  • For the base
  • Mix-ins

Method

  1. 01

    Whisk everything except the Reese's until fully dissolved — no protein-powder clumps, no pudding-mix streaks. A blender works too.

  2. 02

    Pour into a Creami pint, lid on, and freeze flat for 24 hours. Level matters; a tilted pint spins unevenly.

  3. 03

    Spin on Lite Ice Cream. If it comes out crumbly or powdery — it often does — run a re-spin and it turns to soft-serve.

  4. 04

    Dig a well down the center, drop in the chopped Reese's, and run the Mix-In cycle. Eat from the pint; you've earned the macros.
