A 1932 Depression-era quick bread unearthed by B. Dylan Hollis — no butter, no eggs, because there weren't any. Just shelf-stable peanut butter doing all the work. Six ingredients, one bowl, and it makes divine toast.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Oven to 325°F. Grease a standard loaf pan. Whisk the flour, sugar, all four teaspoons of baking powder, and salt in a bowl — we want the bread to rise, not generate thrust, and this is exactly the right amount.

  2. 02

    Stir in the milk until just combined, then add the peanut butter and fold it through — streaks are fine, overmixing is not. There's not much to it. That's the point. It's 1932.

  3. 03

    Scrape into the pan and bake about 1 hour 10 minutes, until deep gold and a skewer comes out clean.

  4. 04

    Cool in the pan 10 minutes, then finish on a rack. Slice thick. Toast it, butter it, or go full circle with more peanut butter and jam.
