Buttermilk cornbread the way a hot cast-iron pan makes it: crackling brown crust, tender middle. The skillet preheats with the oven — the sizzle when the batter hits is the whole point.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Set a 10-inch cast-iron skillet on the middle rack and heat the oven to 425°F. The pan heats with it — give it at least 15 minutes.

  2. 02

    Whisk the cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a big bowl. In another, whisk the buttermilk, eggs, and melted butter until smooth.

  3. 03

    Pour wet into dry and stir just until no dry streaks remain — a few lumps are fine, an overmixed batter bakes up tough.

  4. 04

    Pull the skillet out (it's screaming hot — respect the handle), drop in the 2 tbsp butter, and swirl until foaming. Pour the batter into the sizzle and smooth the top.

  5. 05

    Bake 20–24 minutes, until golden and a toothpick in the center comes out clean. Cool 10 minutes in the pan, then cut into wedges. Serve warm, with honey or more butter — obviously both.
