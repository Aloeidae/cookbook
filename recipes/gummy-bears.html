Chewy little bears in the Haribo style — flavored gelatin for taste and color, extra unflavored gelatin for bite. The chew comes from patience: a day or two of air-drying turns soft jigglers into proper gummies.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Wipe silicone bear molds with the barest film of oil. Stir the water, corn syrup, both gelatins, and citric acid together in a small saucepan and let it bloom 5 minutes — it'll look like grainy cement. That's correct.

  2. 02

    Set the pan over low heat and stir gently until completely melted and clear, 4–5 minutes. Do not let it boil — boiled gelatin loses its set. Skim any foam off the top.

  3. 03

    Working quickly, fill the molds with a dropper or squeeze bottle. If the mixture stiffens mid-pour, rewarm it over low heat for a few seconds.

  4. 04

    Chill 30 minutes, then pop the bears out. They'll be soft — more Jell-O than gummy. Not done yet.

  5. 05

    Spread them on a parchment-lined sheet and air-dry at room temperature 24–48 hours, turning once a day. They shrink, firm up, and get that snappy chew. Store airtight for up to 2 weeks — if they survive the counter that long.
