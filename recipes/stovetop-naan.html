No tandoor, no yeast, no waiting on a rise — yogurt and baking powder do the lifting, a screaming-hot skillet does the rest. The dark blisters are the point, so don't rescue it early.

Ingredients

  • For the dough
  • To finish

Method

  1. 01

    Whisk the flour, sugar, salt, and baking powder in a big bowl. Add the yogurt and oil and work them in with your fingers until the flour looks shaggy.

  2. 02

    Add the warm water a splash at a time, mixing as you go, until you have a dough that's soft but not sticky. You may not need all of it.

  3. 03

    Knead 5–7 minutes until smooth and elastic. Cover the bowl and rest 30 minutes — the dough relaxes, the rolling gets easy.

  4. 04

    Divide into 6 balls. Roll each into a rough oval about ¼ inch thick. Nobody's naan is a perfect teardrop; don't sweat the shape.

  5. 05

    Get a dry skillet properly hot. Cook one naan 1–2 minutes, until bubbles puff across the surface, then flip and go another 1–2 minutes until spotted golden underneath.

  6. 06

    For real char, grab the cooked naan with tongs and flash it over an open flame for a few seconds a side. Brush with the (garlic) butter and stack under a towel while the rest cook.
