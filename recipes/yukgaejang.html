Smoky, spicy, brick-red beef soup packed with shredded brisket, green onions, and gosari — the fernbrake is essential, not optional; it carries the earthy mountain flavor. Serve over rice, with extra salt at the table. Adapted from Maangchi.

Ingredients

  • For the soup
  • For the sauce

Method

  1. 01

    Soak the brisket in cold water for 10–20 minutes, then rinse and drain. Bring the 12 cups of water to a boil in a large pot and drop in the beef, dried shiitakes, and halved onion. Boil over medium-high heat for 1 hour.

  2. 02

    While it boils, mix the sauce — gochugaru, salt, soy sauce, sesame oil, cooking oil — into a loose red paste. Pile the bean sprouts, green onions, gosari, and garlic into your biggest bowl.

  3. 03

    Test the beef: a chunk should split easily with a fork. If it resists, keep going — some briskets take 30 minutes more. Top up with water as it boils off; you want to hold about 10 cups of stock.

  4. 04

    Lift out the beef, mushrooms, and onion. Discard the onion; let the beef and mushrooms cool enough to handle.

  5. 05

    Massage the sauce into the vegetables by hand until every sprout is stained red, then add it all to the boiling stock. Cover and cook 20 minutes — tender, not mushy.

  6. 06

    Slice the mushrooms, pull the beef into strips, and return both to the pot for a final 10 minutes. Ladle over rice, with a small bowl of salt on the table for each person to season their own.
