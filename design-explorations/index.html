Charred leeks with hazelnut & brown butter

  • 6 leeks, trimmed, halved lengthwise
  • 80 g butter
  • 50 g hazelnuts, roughly crushed
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice

Instruction type — 18px light, 1.7 line height

1

Heat a dry cast-iron pan until it just begins to smoke. Lay the leeks cut-side down and leave them alone for six minutes — you want a deep, even char before they ever see the butter.

2

Drop the heat, add the butter, and let it foam through the hazelnuts. Spoon it over the leeks until they relax and gloss.

  • 1.6 kg beef short ribs, bone-in
  • 3 whole star anise
  • 2 strips orange peel, pith removed
  • 500 ml dark stock
Instruction type — 18px Karla, 1.75 line height
1

Season the ribs generously the night before if you can. Brown them hard in a heavy pot — ten unhurried minutes, in batches, until every face is deeply bronzed.

2

Pour off most of the fat, then build the braise: stock, star anise, orange peel. It should come halfway up the ribs, no further.
